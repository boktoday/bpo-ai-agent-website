The four weeks

Learn the job that is growing, not the one that is shrinking

Two sessions a week, 7pm to 9pm Manila time, Tuesday and Thursday. Recorded. Laptop required. Everything taught is free and local-first, so nothing you build depends on a paid subscription.

Week 1

What an agent is

Models, tokens and context in plain words. Instructions that hold, tone in Taglish and English, and your first agent answering your own ten questions.

Week 2

Build it properly

Knowledge bases, tools and guardrails. The agent learns to say “I don't know”, hand over to a human, and refuse what it must refuse.

Week 3

Prove it

Turn twenty real transcripts into a test set. Score it honestly. Red-team day: attack each other's agents, then write a one-page test report.

Week 4

Operate it

Monitoring, incident playbooks, cost per conversation, warm handoffs. Demo day in front of invited employers, with your runbook and report.

Straight talk

Who this is for, and what it is not

The fastest way to lose trust is to look like a recruitment scheme. So here is the plain version.

This is for you if

  • You are or were a call-centre agent, team lead, or virtual assistant.
  • Your work is being automated, or you can see it coming.
  • You have a laptop and stable internet for two evenings a week.
  • You want evidence you can show an employer, not another certificate PDF.
  • You can commit to six of eight sessions and demo day.

This is not

  • A job guarantee. Nobody can promise that honestly.
  • Government accredited, or affiliated with TESDA or IBPAP. Not yet, and we will say so if it changes.
  • A recruitment agency. No fees, no bank details, no placement commissions.
  • A get-rich course. No lambos, no “10x”, no upsell.
  • A place to share anyone's private customer data. Ever.

Where the money goes

Every peso of profit goes to Autism Works

The paid cohorts fund the free ones, and anything left over is donated to the Autism Society Philippines for their Autism Works programme, which has been placing autistic Filipinos into retail, food service and BPO jobs since 2016.

Why this charity

  • Autism Works is an employment programme, not a awareness campaign. It gets people hired.
  • It already places people into BPO roles, the same industry this course serves. The loop closes.
  • Autistic adults are among the most overlooked in Filipino hiring, and agent supervision is work that suits many of them well: pattern, precision, patience.
  • ASP is a national organisation with chapters from Luzon to Mindanao, so the money does not stop in Metro Manila.

The pledge, in writing

  • 100% of surplus, after direct costs (hosting, tools, materials), is donated.
  • Direct costs are published with each donation: what came in, what went out, what was given.
  • Donations are made in the charity's name with the reference published, so anyone can check.
  • If the pilot never makes a profit, that is fine. Cohort one is free and stays free.

Questions

The honest FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Cohort one costs nothing. There is no card field on this page, no deposit, and no upsell at the end.

Do I need to code?

No. You will write instructions and connect tools, which is closer to writing a good SOP than programming. If you can write a clear escalation policy, you can do this.

Will this get me a job?

No guarantee. What it gives you is a working agent, a recorded demo, and a runbook, which is what a hiring manager can actually assess. Employers are being invited to demo day.

Why are you doing this free?

Because I want to learn what the market genuinely needs before charging anyone for it, and because I have family here. The pilot results, including the failures, get published.

What do you do with my data?

Contact you about the cohort, run the pilot, and publish aggregate results. No names, no voice notes, and no contact details in public without your written permission.

What do I need?

A laptop, stable internet, two evenings a week for four weeks, and a willingness to test your own work honestly.
